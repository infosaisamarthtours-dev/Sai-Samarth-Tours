Frequently Asked Questions About Tour Packages from Bangalore

What is included in Sai Samarth Tours flight packages from Bangalore?

Our flight packages include return air tickets from Bangalore, 3-star AC hotel stays, private AC airport and sightseeing transfers, pure vegetarian meals (breakfast, lunch, and dinner), VIP darshan passes, and guidance from an experienced tour manager.

How far in advance should I book Shirdi and Kashi tour packages?

We recommend booking at least 15 to 30 days in advance to secure confirmed flight seats and VIP temple darshan slots, especially for weekend yatras, festive occasions, and school vacation seasons.

Are your tours senior citizen friendly?

Yes! Over 60% of our pilgrims are senior citizens. We offer doorstep assistance, wheelchair arrangements at airports and temple complexes, comfortable AC vehicles with minimal walking distances, and patient tour managers who travel with the group.
